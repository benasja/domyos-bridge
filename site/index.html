Your indoor bike's power, on your Apple Watch

A 3 € ESP32 sits next to the bike, reads its power and cadence over Bluetooth, and shows up on the Watch as a normal power meter. Indoor Cycle workouts then record real watts into Apple Health. No phone app has to stay open.

Install in one click

Plug the ESP32 into this computer with a USB data cable, then:

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. The installer needs a secure (https) page.

Works with ESP32-C3, ESP32-S3 and the classic ESP32. If the board isn't listed after you click, hold BOOT while plugging it in and try again.

What you need

  • An indoor bike that speaks Bluetooth FTMS. Most bikes that work with Zwift or Kinomap do. Tested on a Decathlon Domyos.
  • An ESP32 board. An ESP32-C3 Super Mini (about 3 €) is plenty.
  • An Apple Watch on watchOS 10 or later.
  • A USB charger to keep the board powered next to the bike.

After installing

  1. Power the board near the bike and wake the bike up. It connects on its own and remembers the bike.
  2. On the Watch: Settings → Bluetooth, and under Health Devices tap Domyos Bridge.
  3. Start Workout → Indoor Cycle and pedal. If power and cadence don't show, add those views in the workout's ⋯ → Workout Views.

Close any app that's connected to the bike first (Kinomap, the Domyos app, Zwift). Most bikes allow only one connection.

Open source: github.com/benasja/domyos-bridge. Bug reports and "it works on my bike" reports are both welcome.
